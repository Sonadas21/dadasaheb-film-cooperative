For a multi-state film and entertainment cooperative society, we may want to establish a comprehensive set of committees to address the diverse needs and challenges of operations across different regions. Here’s a suggested list:
1.	Executive Committee
    Provides overall governance and strategic direction.
2.	Finance and Audit Committee
    Manages financial planning, budgeting, and audits.
3.	Production and Content Committee
    Oversees project development, production schedules, and quality control.
4.	Marketing and Public Relations Committee
    Develops marketing strategies and handles public relations efforts.
5.	Membership and Outreach Committee
    Focuses on member recruitment, engagement, and benefits across states.
6.	Legal Affairs and Compliance Committee
    Addresses legal issues, compliance with regulations, and intellectual property rights.
7.	Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion Committee
    Promotes inclusive practices and representation within the organization.
8.	Education and Training Committee
    Organizes workshops, seminars, and training programs for skill development.
9.	Technology and Innovation Committee
    Evaluates and implements new technologies and practices in production.
10.	Events and Festivals Committee
    Plans and coordinates events, screenings, and film festivals.
11.	Sustainability and Environmental Practices Committee
    Promotes eco-friendly practices in film production and operations.
12.	Community Engagement Committee
    Builds relationships with local communities and stakeholders.
13.	Grants and Funding Committee
    Identifies funding opportunities and manages grant applications.
14.	Regional Development Committee
    Focuses on addressing specific needs and opportunities in different states.
These committees can help ensure effective governance, foster collaboration, and support the diverse interests of members across multiple states.
